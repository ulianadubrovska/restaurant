How Tammy Food works

From exploring the menu to building your own dish and letting AI cook for you — the whole Tammy Food experience in three simple steps.

Explore the menu

Scroll through “Our Delicious Dish”, check photos, prices and ratings, switch pages, and add your favourite meals to the cart in one click.

Build your own dish

Use the dish constructor: choose base, protein, veggies, sauces and drinks. Price and calories update instantly, so you always see your perfect combo.

Let AI cook for you

Describe your taste profile, and the AI chef generates a detailed recipe and cooking method. Save the result to favourites and repeat your masterpiece next time in just a few clicks.
